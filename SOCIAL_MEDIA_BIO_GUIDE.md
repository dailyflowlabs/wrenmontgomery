#### 5. Long-Form Bio / Extended Story (For Website / Press Kits / Facebook Note)
```text
Hailing from the rolling cedar hills and creek beds of Leiper's Fork, Tennessee, 22-year-old Wren Montgomery is bringing infectious neo-traditional grit, razor-sharp Southern wit, and stadium-sized hooks back to country music. 

Raised on red-dirt back roads with grease on her knuckles and three-part church harmonies in her soul, Wren can tear down a small-block carburetor with her dad just as easily as she can write an anthemic radio singalong. Armed with her signature wildflower sleeve tattoo, sun-bleached beach waves, and an acoustic guitar that’s survived more bonfires than boardrooms, she blends the swagger of Lainey Wilson and the conversational charm of Megan Moroney with high-energy modern bounce.
```
