The Heritage

FROM LEIPER'S FORK TO NASHVILLE

Wren Montgomery didn't buy her boots at a tourist shop on Broadway. Raised on red-dirt gravel roads and creek beds in Leiper's Fork, Tennessee, she grew up tearing down small-block engines with her dad and singing three-part church harmonies on Sunday mornings.

With her signature botanical wildflower sleeve, sun-bleached beach waves, and an acoustic guitar that’s survived more bonfires than boardrooms, Wren pairs 90s country attitude with modern stadium pop hooks.

Her debut single "Ain't My First Rodeo (Just My First Clown)" is the anthem for anyone who’s ever let a charming cowboy talk big, only to hand him his hat on the way out the screen door. Driving telecaster chicken-pickin', snappy handclaps, and a punchline chorus you can't get out of your head.
